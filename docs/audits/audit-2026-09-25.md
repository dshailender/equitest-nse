### Product Owner Narrative
The EquiTest NSE codebase demonstrates rigorous engineering discipline across its lower-level mathematical mechanics: indicator computation matches TradingView golden references within $10^{-6}$, overnight gap-down stop loss execution accurately models market fills without unrealizable price improvement, capital constraints strictly cap portfolio exposure to ~3–4 concurrent positions, and PDF exports are 100% byte-deterministic. Furthermore, the test suites pass 100% (147 backend unit tests, 30 Vitest tests, 18 Playwright E2E tests).

However, from an institutional financial research perspective, the system currently cannot produce credible backtest results for its intended universe. When a simulation is triggered via the UI or API, the execution worker hardcodes a fallback to a 3-stock synthetic test fixture (`['ALPHA', 'BETA', 'GAMMA']`), producing exactly 2 trades across a 5-year backtest. The constituent universe contains dummy placeholder tokens (`MIDCAP_STOCK_101..750`) rather than genuine NSE equities, historical point-in-time universe filtering is never invoked during the simulation loop, 10-year and 15-year simulations silently truncate to a 500-day window while presenting annualizations over the truncated span, and large-cap Nifty 50 stocks (which are explicitly forbidden from the strategy) appear as demo presets across the user interface. Until these 1 Blocker and 5 Critical defects are remediated, the platform cannot be defended before an institutional risk committee.

## Findings by Domain

### DOMAIN A — Does the backtest actually work?
The backtesting core engine executes deterministically and respects order mechanics (T+1 market open execution, 7% stop-loss from entry, 10 bps slippage per side, and overnight gap-down resolution). However, because the backend execution dispatcher hardcodes a check for `tiny_universe/ALPHA.parquet`, any backtest initiated without specifying symbols defaults to `['ALPHA', 'BETA', 'GAMMA']`. Over a 5-year run (2019–2024), this produces only 2 closed trades total (both in ALPHA: one exit due to an overnight gap-down at -10.75%, and one exit due to EMA20 crossover at -1.40%). 10-year and 15-year backtests silently execute on the exact same 500-day window, producing identical outputs.

- **AUD-A-001**: Fix backtest simulation defaulting to mock synthetic universe (ALPHA, BETA, GAMMA) instead of NSE 101–750 [Blocker]
- **AUD-A-002**: Prevent silent truncation of 10-year and 15-year backtest date ranges when historical data is absent [Critical]

### DOMAIN B — Universe: NSE 101–750 and missing stock names
`GET /api/v1/universe` returns 650 constituent items for ranks 101–750. However, the API returns only raw ticker strings without human-readable company names, sectors, or market capitalization. Furthermore, the stored fixture (`constituents.parquet`) uses synthetic dummy strings `MIDCAP_STOCK_101` through `MIDCAP_STOCK_750` rather than authentic NSE tickers. For dates prior to 2020, the fallback algorithm generates fictitious cycle tokens (`IDEA_1`, `YESBANK_1`). On the frontend, `/data` renders an unpaginated, unsearchable badge dump.

- **AUD-B-001**: Add human-readable stock names to universe constituent API and UI views [Major]
- **AUD-B-002**: Replace synthetic dummy symbols (MIDCAP_STOCK_101..750) with authentic historical NSE constituent mappings [Critical]

### DOMAIN C — Stocks outside the universe appearing on the frontend
Large-cap stocks ranked 1–100 (RELIANCE, HDFCBANK, INFY, TATAMOTORS) appear as default presets on `/signals`, `/indicators`, and `/validation`, directly contradicting REQ-1.1 which excludes large-caps. In the backend, `Backtest` is instantiated without a `universe_provider`, which causes `Backtest.run()` to freeze candidate selection to the startup price keys rather than evaluating active constituents on each session T-1.

- **AUD-C-001**: Remove Large-Cap (Nifty 50) presets from /signals, /indicators, and /validation pages [Major]
- **AUD-C-002**: Inject point-in-time universe provider into Backtest engine during simulation run [Critical]

### DOMAIN D — Frontend responsiveness and fluidity
Automated Playwright testing across five standard viewports (375px, 768px, 1024px, 1440px, 1920px) revealed severe horizontal layout overflow on mobile (375px: +517px scrollWidth) and tablet (768px: +132px scrollWidth) across all 9 application pages due to rigid navigation and grid container sizing. In addition, an automated axe-core accessibility audit identified 23 WCAG 2.1 AA violations across the application, including unlabelled icon buttons, form inputs missing linked `<label>` elements, and color contrast failures.

- **AUD-D-001**: Fix severe horizontal overflow on mobile (375px) and tablet (768px) viewports across all pages [Major]
- **AUD-D-002**: Remediate WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility violations (missing button names, unassociated form labels, low contrast) [Major]

### DOMAIN E — Data availability for 5, 10, and 15 years
The SQLite database stores coverage for only 5 symbols over a 5-year window (2019-01-01 to 2024-01-01, 1,305 trading sessions). 10-year and 15-year continuous historical data does not exist in the database or in offline fixtures for the 650 universe constituents. While the NIFTY 50 benchmark has 15+ years of data in the offline fixture, individual equities lack multi-decade coverage. Corporate action adjustments (`adj_close` vs `close`) were verified to be mathematically continuous for RELIANCE.

- **AUD-E-001**: Only 5 symbols stored in SQLite coverage database covering 5 years (15-year data coverage absent) [Critical]

### DOMAIN F — Backtest engine gaps (Senior Lead QA lens)
The backtesting mathematical core passed several stringent QA tests: price truncation guards prevent look-ahead bias, EMA-200 warmup prevents spurious early signals, stop-loss and gap-down rules match specifications, and runs are 100% deterministic across multiple executions. However, switching `DATA_SOURCE=yfinance` causes an immediate `AttributeError: 'YFinanceSource' object has no attribute 'get_coverage'`, and the cost model lumps statutory fees (STT, GST, stamp duty) into a symmetric 10 bps slippage without tax itemization.

- **AUD-F-001**: YFinanceSource lacks get_coverage() implementation causing AttributeError when switched [Critical]
- **AUD-F-002**: Lack of explicit transaction cost itemization (STT, GST, Exchange charges lumped into slippage) [Minor]

### DOMAIN G — Reports and exports
CSV, XLSX, and PDF exports generate successfully and exhibit cross-format metric agreement. The PDF generation engine is 100% bit-for-bit deterministic when `include_creation_date=False`, correctly normalizes timestamps, and renders institutional A4 styling with dynamic running headers and footers. However, when trade count exceeds 5,000, rows are capped without a dynamic count indicator in table footers.

- **AUD-G-001**: PDF trade log silently caps at 5,000 trades without indication of omitted rows in table footer [Minor]

### DOMAIN H — PO / research credibility lens
A quantitative research analyst cannot present the current report to a risk committee because it lacks benchmark relative performance analytics. The system does not calculate Benchmark CAGR, Strategy Alpha, Beta, or Information Ratio, nor does it plot an overlaid Nifty 50 benchmark curve on the equity chart. Parameter sensitivity sweeps (SL 5%, 6%, 7%) demonstrate stable behavior without knife-edge collapse, and audit provenance logging captures git commit and data hashes.

- **AUD-H-001**: Reports lack NIFTY benchmark buy-and-hold comparison and alpha/beta analytics [Major]
- **AUD-H-002**: Handoff Section 2 base SHA mismatch with git HEAD [Minor]

## Traceability Gaps

Cross-referencing the implemented system against `TRACEABILITY.md` and the PRD requirements revealed the following operational gaps:

1. **REQ-1.1 (Universe Selection NSE 101–750)**:
   - While `backend/app/data/universe.py` provides rank filtering logic, the fixture data (`constituents.parquet`) uses synthetic dummy strings `MIDCAP_STOCK_101..750` rather than genuine NSE stocks.
   - When executing backtests via the API, the universe filter is bypassed in favor of `['ALPHA', 'BETA', 'GAMMA']`.
2. **REQ-0.1 (Survivorship Bias Prevention)**:
   - Point-in-time constituent resolution is implemented in `get_universe()`, but is **never passed** to `Backtest(universe_provider=...)`. Simulation runs freeze universe membership to startup keys.
3. **REQ-5.1 (Simulation Engine)**:
   - The engine is tested and certified on `tiny_universe`, but full-scale execution across the 650-stock universe cannot run due to missing multi-symbol price fixtures.
4. **REQ-7.1 (Performance Metrics Engine)**:
   - PRD benchmark comparison metrics (Benchmark Return, Strategy Alpha, Beta) are completely missing from `PerformanceMetrics` and report dashboards.

## Things That Looked Wrong But Are Actually Correct

To demonstrate audit rigor and prevent false-positive defect reporting, the following behaviors were investigated and confirmed to be correct per `docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md` and `TRACEABILITY.md`:

1. **Trade Realized Loss Exceeding 7% on Stop Loss Exit**:
   - *Observation*: Trade #1 in the backtest log suffered a realized loss of -10.75%, despite the strategy specifying a 7% stop loss.
   - *Verification*: Per ASSUMPTIONS.md Open Question #2 and Section 0.2, if a stock gaps down overnight below the stop loss price ($\text{Open}_T \le \text{SL Price}$), the order fills at Market Open rather than the stop loss price. Realized losses exceed 7% to prevent unrealizable price improvement during gap-down market openings. This is correct behavior.
2. **Portfolio Limited to 3 Concurrent Positions**:
   - *Observation*: During periods with multiple valid entry signals, the engine never holds more than 3 positions concurrently.
   - *Verification*: Per ASSUMPTIONS.md Section 5.3, with a starting corpus of ₹5,00,000, 2% risk, and 7% SL, each trade requires ₹1,42,800 capital ($\approx 28.56\%$ allocation). Three positions consume 85.7% of corpus; a 4th position requires more capital than available free cash and is legitimately rejected with `reason: "insufficient_capital"`. This is correct behavior.
3. **Zero Trades in First 200 Bars of Symbol History**:
   - *Observation*: No entry signals or trades occur during the first 200 trading sessions of any symbol.
   - *Verification*: Per ASSUMPTIONS.md Section 2.1, all 4 EMAs require valid values, and EMA-200 requires 200 sessions to warm up (`min_periods=200`). All trend and regime filters evaluate to `False` until bar 200. This is correct behavior.
4. **Deterministic PDF Bit-for-Bit SHA-256 Hashes**:
   - *Observation*: Two consecutively generated PDF files for the same run produced identical SHA-256 byte hashes.
   - *Verification*: Phase 9 explicitly strips dynamic metadata (`/CreationDate`, `/ModDate`, `/ID`) with `pypdf` and fixes `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH=0` during chart and PDF rendering. This is intentional and verified correct.
5. **Exact Match to TradingView EMA Within $10^{-6}$**:
   - *Observation*: Verification on RELIANCE EMA-20 matches TradingView historical series to within $10^{-6}$.
   - *Verification*: The pandas exponential moving average uses $\alpha = 2 / (span + 1)$ with `adjust=False`, exactly replicating standard TA-Lib / TradingView algorithms.
